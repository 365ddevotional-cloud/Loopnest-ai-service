PART 6 — EMAIL ERROR HANDLING AND DELIVERY LOGGING
==================================================

Do not silently swallow email errors.

Email failure must not reverse or cancel:

- account creation
- organization-registration creation
- compliance-case creation
- approval decisions

When an email fails:

- keep the underlying action successful when appropriate
- save a safe email-delivery status
- log the provider response without secrets
- show the platform admin a clear warning

Example:

“The action was completed, but the email notification could not be sent. The recipient can still see the in-app notification.”

Track these statuses where practical:

- queued
- accepted by provider
- sent
- failed
- skipped because no verified email
- skipped because consent or policy prevents delivery

Do not claim inbox delivery merely because the provider accepted the request.
