====================================================
1. RESTRICT SUSPENDED, REJECTED, OR INACTIVE ORGANIZATIONS
====================================================

Organizations with any of these statuses must not accept new members:

- suspended
- rejected
- inactive
- archived
- pending deletion
- permanently removed

While restricted, block:

- creation of new invitation codes
- use of existing invitation codes
- new membership requests
- approval of pending membership requests
- creation of new departments or groups
- public organization discovery
- public organization join links
- organization-wide announcements to ordinary members, where suspension rules require it

The server must enforce these restrictions.

Do not rely only on hidden or disabled frontend buttons.

Invitation verification must return a clear message such as:

“This organization is not currently accepting new members.”

Do not reveal confidential compliance details to the person attempting to join.

====================================================
2. ACCESS THAT MUST REMAIN AVAILABLE DURING SUSPENSION
====================================================

A suspended organization owner must still be able to:

- sign in
- see the organization’s current compliance status
- read private messages from the Platform Administrator
- open compliance cases
- respond to allegations or requests
- upload supporting documents
- view required actions and deadlines
- submit an appeal where allowed
- view final decisions
- request clarification
- access permitted account records
- request an export of their organization’s data where supported

Do not completely lock the owner out of the account, because they need access to respond and appeal.

Ordinary members may see a neutral notice if their organization is temporarily unavailable, but they must never see private allegations, internal evidence, owner responses, or administrator notes.

====================================================
3. END-TO-END COMPLIANCE CASE WORKFLOW
====================================================

Test and complete this full workflow:

1. Platform Administrator opens a compliance case.
2. A unique case number is created.
3. The organization owner receives a private in-app notice.
4. The owner receives an email notification where email delivery is available.
5. The owner opens the case.
6. The owner reads:
   - the allegation or concern
   - the relevant policy
   - requested action
   - response deadline
   - possible consequences
   - appeal information
7. The owner submits a response.
8. The owner may upload supporting documents or evidence.
9. Platform Administrator reviews the response.
10. Administrator may:
    - request more information
    - issue no action
    - issue a warning
    - request corrective action
    - temporarily restrict the organization
    - temporarily suspend the organization
    - resolve the case
    - permanently remove the organization where justified
11. The owner receives the decision privately.
12. The resolution is saved in the audit history.
13. If permitted, the owner may submit an appeal.
14. The appeal is reviewed and a final decision is recorded.
15. The owner receives confirmation when the case is resolved or closed.

Case statuses should include:

- Open
- Waiting for Owner Response
- Waiting for Platform Review
- More Information Requested
- Corrective Action Required
- Under Appeal
- Resolved
- Closed
- Suspended
- Permanently Removed

====================================================
4. FAIR AND CAREFUL LANGUAGE
====================================================

Until a matter is decided, do not present an allegation as a proven fact.

Use language such as:

- “We received a report alleging…”
- “A concern has been raised regarding…”
- “This matter is currently under review.”
- “Please provide your response by…”

Avoid language such as:

- “You committed fraud”
- “You violated the law”
- “You are guilty”

unless the platform has reached a documented decision and the wording has been approved for that decision.

Include a notice that:

- the message is confidential
- it is intended for the organization owner
- the owner should respond through the secure case page
- retaliation against a reporter or member is prohibited where relevant

5. REAL EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS FOR IMPORTANT CASE EVENTS
====================================================

Send email notifications to the organization owner for important compliance events, including:

- compliance case opened
- additional information requested
- response deadline approaching
- response received
- warning issued
- corrective action required
- temporary restriction applied
- suspension applied
- suspension lifted
- case resolved
- case closed
- appeal received
- appeal decided
- organization application approved
- organization application rejected
- organization deletion request updated

Requirements:

- Send only to the organization owner’s registered email.
- Do not expose member, reporter, administrator, or other organization email addresses.
- Use the existing approved email-delivery system.
- Do not expose secrets in frontend code.
- Record whether the email was:
  - queued
  - sent
  - failed
- Record the provider response or safe error code.
- Do not claim delivery if the provider only accepted the request.
- Keep the full private notice available in-app even if email delivery fails.
- Avoid placing highly sensitive evidence directly in the email; direct the owner to sign in securely.

Email subject examples:

- Action Required: Compliance Case [Case Number]
- Additional Information Requested
- Organization Status Updated
- Compliance Case Resolved
- Appeal Decision Available

Support English, Spanish, and French.

====================================================
6. RESPONSE DEADLINES AND REMINDERS
====================================================

Allow the Platform Administrator to assign a response deadline.

Examples:

- 24 hours
- 48 hours
- 7 days
- custom date and time

The owner must see:

- exact deadline
- time remaining
- required action
- consequences of no response, stated carefully

Send reminders where appropriate:

- when the case opens
- 24 hours before the deadline
- shortly before expiration where practical
- after the deadline passes

Missing a deadline must not automatically cause permanent deletion.

The Platform Administrator must review the case before major enforcement, except where urgent safety, legal, fraud, or security concerns justify immediate temporary restriction.
