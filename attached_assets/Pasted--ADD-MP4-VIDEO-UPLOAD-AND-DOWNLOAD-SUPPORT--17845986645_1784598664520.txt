==================================================
ADD MP4 VIDEO UPLOAD AND DOWNLOAD SUPPORT
==================================================

In addition to the existing audio support, allow each song to have an OPTIONAL MP4 video file.

Do not remove, replace, or disrupt the existing audio upload, playback, or download system.

--------------------------------------------------
ADMIN SONG FORM
--------------------------------------------------

For every song upload and edit form, add:

Video File — optional

Accepted format:

• MP4 only

Add separate controls:

Audio Download:
• Enabled
• Disabled

Video Download:
• Enabled
• Disabled

Default for new songs:

• Audio Download Enabled
• Video Download Enabled only when an MP4 file has been uploaded

If no video file exists:

• Hide or disable the Video Download setting
• Do not show a broken video button publicly

In the admin song list, clearly display:

• Audio Download Enabled / Disabled
• Video Download Enabled / Disabled
• MP4 Uploaded / No Video

The admin must be able to:

• Upload an MP4 while creating a song
• Add or replace an MP4 later
• Remove the MP4 without deleting the audio or song record
• Enable or disable audio and video downloads independently

Do not automatically generate or convert an MP4 from the audio file.

--------------------------------------------------
MULTI-SONG BATCH UPLOAD
--------------------------------------------------

For batch uploads, allow each selected song entry to have:

• One audio file
• One optional MP4 video file
• One optional cover image
• Separate Audio Download and Video Download settings

Keep each MP4 correctly attached to its matching song.

Do not require every song in the batch to have a video.

Use controlled uploads so large MP4 files do not overload the server.

Recommended maximum concurrency:

• Two total file uploads at a time

Show separate progress indicators for:

• Audio upload
• Video upload
• Cover image upload

If the MP4 upload fails but the audio succeeds:

• Preserve the uploaded audio and song record
• Mark the video upload as Failed
• Allow Retry Video
• Do not duplicate the song

--------------------------------------------------
PUBLIC SONG PAGE
--------------------------------------------------

When a song has downloadable audio and video, show two clear buttons:

• Download Audio
• Download Video (MP4)

Do not combine both into one unclear button.

When only audio is available:

• Show Download Audio
• Do not show Download Video

When only video is available:

• Show Download Video (MP4)
• Do not show Download Audio

When both are disabled:

Show:

Download Unavailable

When the user taps either download button, open the existing download encouragement modal.

Update the modal title according to the selected format:

For audio:
Your Audio Download Is Ready

For video:
Your Video Download Is Ready

Keep the existing voluntary-support message and Scripture:

“Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.”
— 2 Corinthians 9:7

Keep this statement visible:

“Giving is completely optional. You may continue your download whether or not you give.”

For audio, use:

Continue to Download Audio

For MP4, use:

Continue to Download Video

The support button must continue opening the existing donation system.

--------------------------------------------------
VIDEO DOWNLOAD BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------

When Video Download is enabled:

• Download or open the original uploaded MP4 file
• Preserve the original video quality
• Use a safe filename based on the song title
• Example:
  the-lord-is-my-shepherd.mp4
• Do not transcode or compress the MP4
• Do not duplicate it in storage
• Do not expose private server paths
• Use the existing storage URL or secure download endpoint

On iPhone Safari, if the browser opens the video instead of saving it, display:

“Tap the Share icon, then choose Save to Files or Save Video.”

Do not claim that the video was downloaded unless the browser initiated or opened the file.

--------------------------------------------------
FILE VALIDATION AND COST CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------

Validate before uploading:

• File extension is .mp4
• MIME type is video/mp4
• File size is within the current storage/server limit

Use the project’s existing storage system only.

Do not add:

• Video conversion
• FFmpeg
• Automatic rendering
• Streaming infrastructure
• A paid video service
• A new storage subscription

If there is no existing MP4 size limit, use a conservative limit that the current server safely supports and clearly show the limit beside the upload field.

Reject unsupported video formats with a clear message:

“Please upload an MP4 video file.”

Do not silently compress or alter the video.

--------------------------------------------------
PRESERVE CURRENT FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------

Do not break:

• Existing audio files
• Audio playback
• Existing song records
• Existing cover images
• Lyrics
• Credits
• Favorites
• Library
• Sharing
• Donations
• Admin navigation
• Song testimonials
• Existing batch uploads

--------------------------------------------------
TEST
--------------------------------------------------

Test:

1. Upload a song with audio only.
2. Upload a song with audio and MP4.
3. Upload multiple songs where only some have MP4 files.
4. Confirm audio and video remain attached to the correct song.
5. Confirm Download Audio works.
6. Confirm Download Video works.
7. Confirm no video button appears when no MP4 exists.
8. Confirm disabling video download hides or disables only the video button.
9. Confirm audio playback remains unchanged.
10. Confirm a failed video upload does not remove a successful audio upload.

Return additionally:

• Video database field added, if any
• MP4 storage path or existing storage bucket used
• Maximum MP4 file size allowed
• Audio and video download test results
