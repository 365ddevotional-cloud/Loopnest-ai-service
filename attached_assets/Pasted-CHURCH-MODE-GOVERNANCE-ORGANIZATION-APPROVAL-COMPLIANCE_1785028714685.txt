CHURCH MODE GOVERNANCE, ORGANIZATION APPROVAL, COMPLIANCE, LEGAL SAFEGUARDS, AND PLATFORM COMMUNICATION

Implement the following improvements for Church Mode.

IMPORTANT

- Do not modify unrelated app features.
- Preserve all existing organizations, churches, ministries, departments, members, invitations, and data.
- Reuse the existing authentication, notification, email, translation, and messaging systems wherever possible.
- Make all changes secure, auditable, scalable, and professionally designed.
- Do not remove any existing functionality unless explicitly stated.

====================================================
1. ORGANIZATION APPROVAL SYSTEM
====================================================

Every organization that creates an official community inside Church Mode must be reviewed before becoming publicly active.

This applies equally to:

• Churches
• Christian Ministries
• Christian Organizations
• Christian Associations
• Fellowships
• Mission Organizations
• Bible Study Organizations
• Campus Ministries
• Prayer Networks
• Other Christian Communities approved by the platform.

Ordinary members joining an existing organization do NOT require platform approval.

Workflow:

Draft

↓

Submitted

↓

Pending Platform Review

↓

Approved

or

Needs More Information

or

Rejected

While Pending Review:

• The organization is not publicly discoverable.
• Invitation codes remain inactive.
• Members cannot join.
• Public pages remain hidden.
• The owner may continue editing information.

Display:

"Your organization is currently under review by the platform."

====================================================
2. PLATFORM REVIEW
====================================================

Platform Administrators review:

• Organization name
• Organization type
• Denomination (if applicable)
• Description
• Country
• Contact details
• Logo
• Website
• Any required supporting information

Administrators may:

• Approve
• Reject
• Request Additional Information

Every action must be recorded in the audit log.

====================================================
3. PLATFORM APPROVAL IS NOT AN ENDORSEMENT
====================================================

IMPORTANT

Never display:

✓ Verified Church
✓ Certified Church
✓ Official Church

Instead display neutral wording such as:

• Approved for Church Mode
• Platform Approved
• Community Approved

Approval means ONLY that the organization satisfied the platform's submission requirements.

Approval DOES NOT mean:

• endorsement
• accreditation
• certification
• theological approval
• financial approval
• legal guarantee
• guarantee of future conduct

This explanation should appear in the Terms of Use and relevant policy pages.

====================================================
4. PRIVATE PLATFORM ADMIN ↔ ORGANIZATION OWNER COMMUNICATION
====================================================

Create a secure communication channel between:

Platform Administrator

and

Organization Owner

Only those two parties may see these messages.

Topics include:

• policy clarification
• compliance
• abuse reports
• impersonation
• copyright
• inappropriate content
• fraud prevention
• legal requests
• account verification
• organization review

No ordinary member may view these conversations.

====================================================
5. COMPLIANCE CASES
====================================================

Instead of warnings, create Compliance Cases.

Each case stores:

• Case Number
• Organization
• Owner
• Date
• Status
• Category
• Severity
• Description
• Attachments
• Internal Notes
• Owner Responses
• Resolution History

Statuses include:

Open

Waiting for Owner

Under Review

Resolved

Closed

Suspended

====================================================
6. FAIR REVIEW PROCESS
====================================================

Never assume wrongdoing.

Always use language such as:

"We received a report alleging..."

instead of

"You violated..."

The owner must have an opportunity to respond before permanent enforcement whenever practical and legally appropriate.

====================================================
7. OWNER RESPONSE
====================================================

Organization owners may:

• submit explanations
• upload documents
• upload evidence
• communicate privately with Platform Administration

Responses become part of the permanent audit history.

====================================================
8. ENFORCEMENT OPTIONS
====================================================

Platform Administrators may choose:

No Action

Warning

Request Changes

Temporary Restriction

Temporary Suspension

Permanent Removal

Every action requires:

• reason
• administrator
• timestamp

====================================================
9. APPEALS
====================================================

Organization owners may submit an appeal after rejection or suspension.

Appeals become part of the audit history.

====================================================
10. ORGANIZATION DELETION
====================================================

Organization owners may NOT permanently delete their own organization.

Instead they submit:

Request Organization Deletion

The request includes:

• reason
• confirmation
• optional explanation

Status:

Pending Review

The platform reviews the request.

Target review time:

Within 48 hours.

Deletion is never automatic.

Platform Administrators decide whether to:

• approve
• reject
• archive
• suspend instead

Prefer:

Disable

↓

Suspend

↓

Archive

↓

Delete

====================================================
11. LEGAL SAFEGUARDS
====================================================

Create professionally written legal policies explaining:

• Organizations remain solely responsible for their teachings, publications, donations, fundraising, events, counseling, communications, leadership, and activities.

• The platform provides technology only.

• Platform approval is not an endorsement or certification.

• The platform may investigate reports fairly.

• Organizations may respond before enforcement where practical.

• The platform may suspend or remove organizations violating platform policies or applicable laws.

• Records may be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and audit purposes.

• Platform Administrators reserve the right to review applications and moderate participation according to published policies.

Use language consistent with modern app-store expectations and generally accepted platform governance practices.
