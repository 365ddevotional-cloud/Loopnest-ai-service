8. IMPROVE READABILITY (HIGH PRIORITY)
==================================================

The current font sizes throughout the donation experience are too small and difficult to read on mobile.

Increase readability across BOTH:

• The DonationModal (opened from song pages)
• The /donate page

Requirements:

• Increase the base font size throughout the donation experience.
• Use larger, bold headings.
• Increase paragraph font size.
• Increase button text size.
• Increase spacing between paragraphs.
• Increase line height for easier reading.
• Increase padding inside cards.
• Make important information (account number, bank name, Venmo username, instructions) bold and highly visible.
• Ensure text remains responsive on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Typography should feel similar to modern reading apps such as ChatGPT:
- Large
- Clean
- Bold where appropriate
- High contrast
- Easy to scan
- Comfortable for older users

Suggested minimum sizes:

Main Heading:
34–38px
Weight: Bold (700–800)

Section Titles:
24–28px
Weight: Bold

Body Text:
18–20px
Weight: 400–500

Important Information:
20–22px
Weight: Bold

Buttons:
18–20px
Weight: Bold

Labels:
18px

Increase button height and touch targets to at least 48px.

Do NOT simply zoom the page.
Use proper typography styling.
