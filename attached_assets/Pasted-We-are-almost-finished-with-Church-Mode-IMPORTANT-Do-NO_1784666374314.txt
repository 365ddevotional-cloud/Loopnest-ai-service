3. PLATFORM SERVICE FEE
==================================================

Money must NEVER pass through the 365 Daily Devotional account.

The church receives funds directly.

The platform receives only the configured service fee.

Display the calculation transparently.

Example:

Donation: $100

Platform Fee: $2

Church Receives: $98

The platform fee percentage must only be editable by the Super Admin.

Church admins can see the percentage but cannot modify it.
