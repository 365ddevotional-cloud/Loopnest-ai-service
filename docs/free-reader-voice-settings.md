# Free device voice settings

Owner chose to keep the existing browser/device reader and stop the ElevenLabs purchase. No voice subscription was purchased; paid narration remains off. This change calls no provider, creates no cloud resources and does not generate or clone a voice.

Settings previously listed English voices only. The voice section now offers the six interface languages, lists only compatible installed voices, previews a short phrase in the selected language, and stores a separate preference for each language. The original English storage key and Scripture preview remain compatible. A French preference cannot overwrite the English choice. Pidgin prefers a dedicated voice, then Nigerian English, then other English voices and explicitly identifies that fallback.

Automatic selection prefers voices labelled Natural, Premium, Enhanced or Neural. Existing saved choices take precedence. A baseline Google name no longer outranks the device default merely because of its provider name. Voice names cannot guarantee natural quality; the Test button is the audition. Unsupported languages show a message and disable the sample button. Refresh voices rereads the device list after an installed voice changes; the app does not install voices. Public reader errors and the explicit English-original fallback remain available. Missing Yoruba/Hausa voices are not silently replaced with English.

Preview now uses the shared reader, including its retained utterance, paused-queue recovery, language handling and startup errors. The phone menu includes an Audio Voice entry that opens a focused voice dialog. The settings dialog scrolls within 90% of the viewport to keep the added controls accessible on phones.

Tests cover independent language preferences, existing English choices, refreshed voice discovery, language-matched preview, unavailable Yoruba controls and the unchanged English Scripture phrase. Mock voices do not certify natural sound on the owner's iPhone.
