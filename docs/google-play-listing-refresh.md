## Short description (under 80 characters)

## Full description (under 4,000 characters)

Begin each day with Scripture and a devotional message that invites reflection, prayer, and faith.

365 Daily Devotional brings daily Christian encouragement together with Bible reading, past messages, prayer requests, and worship music. Read today's message, return to a published devotional in the archive, or spend time with prayer points and declarations.

What you can do:

• Read daily Scripture-based devotionals and explore published messages by date.
• Read the Bible in the app and choose from available public-domain translations.
• Use prayer points and faith declarations to guide personal reflection.
• Send a prayer or counseling request through the app and review the form's privacy choices.
• Listen to worship songs and watch available song videos.
• Use the quick tour and How to Use guide when you need help finding a feature.

Some music downloads are optional purchases. The price and access period are shown before checkout. The devotional and listening experience is available without buying a download.

365 Daily Devotional is intended for spiritual encouragement. Prayer and counseling requests are not an emergency service or a substitute for professional medical, legal, or mental health care.
